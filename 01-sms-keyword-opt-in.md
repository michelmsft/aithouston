# SMS / Keyword Opt-In Instructions

## AIT-Houston SMS Notifications by Mostofit LLC

AIT-Houston members may opt in to receive SMS notifications by texting the approved keyword to the AIT-Houston SMS number.

## How to Opt In by SMS

To subscribe, a user sends the keyword:

**JOIN**

to the AIT-Houston SMS phone number.

After sending the keyword, the user receives a confirmation message explaining the type of messages they will receive.

## Example Confirmation Message

Thank you for opting in to receive AIT-Houston SMS notifications from Mostofit LLC. Messages may include account verification, announcements, event reminders, donation confirmations, and urgent community updates. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

## Message Types

Subscribers may receive:

- Account verification codes
- Community announcements
- Event reminders
- Donation confirmations
- Emergency or urgent notifications
- Member communication updates

## Opt-Out Instructions

Users may unsubscribe at any time by replying:

**STOP**

## Help Instructions

Users may request help at any time by replying:

**HELP**

## Support

For support, contact Mostofit LLC or AIT-Houston through the official app or website.
