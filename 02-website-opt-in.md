# Website Opt-In Instructions

## AIT-Houston SMS Notification Consent

Users may opt in to SMS notifications through the AIT-Houston website or mobile app registration form.

## How Users Provide Consent

During registration, users provide their phone number and select a consent checkbox agreeing to receive SMS notifications from AIT-Houston.

## Required Consent Language

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS notifications from AIT-Houston and Mostofit LLC regarding announcements, events, account activity, donation confirmations, verification codes, and community updates. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

## Information Collected

The opt-in form may collect:

- First name
- Last name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Membership or account information
- SMS consent confirmation

## Message Types

SMS messages may include:

- Account verification codes
- Event reminders
- Community announcements
- Donation confirmations
- Emergency notifications
- Member updates

## Opt-Out

Users can opt out at any time by replying **STOP** to any SMS message.

## Help

Users can reply **HELP** for assistance.
