# Additional Supporting Documentation

## Program Overview

Mostofit LLC supports SMS messaging for the AIT-Houston mobile application and related digital services. AIT-Houston is a community engagement platform for members of the Association des Ivoiriens du Texas - Houston.

## Purpose of SMS Messaging

SMS messaging is used only for communication with registered users who have provided consent.

Messages may include:

- Account verification codes
- Member announcements
- Event reminders
- Donation confirmations
- Community updates
- Urgent or emergency notifications
- Administrative account notifications

## Consent

Users must voluntarily provide their phone number and agree to receive SMS notifications before messages are sent. Consent may be collected through the mobile app, website registration form, or SMS keyword opt-in.

## No Third-Party Marketing

AIT-Houston and Mostofit LLC do not use this SMS program to send third-party marketing messages. SMS communications are limited to account, community, event, donation, and member-related notifications.

## Opt-Out

Users can opt out at any time by replying **STOP** to any SMS message.

## Help

Users can reply **HELP** to receive support instructions.

## Example SMS Disclosure

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.
