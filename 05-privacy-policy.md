# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-05-27

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mostofit LLC collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided through the AIT-Houston mobile application, website, and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Account information
- Membership information
- Donation and event participation information
- Device and app usage information
- SMS notification preferences

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Verify user identity
- Send account notifications
- Send event reminders and announcements
- Process donation-related confirmations
- Improve app functionality
- Communicate important community updates
- Provide customer support

## 4. SMS Communications

By providing a phone number and opting in, users agree to receive SMS messages from AIT-Houston and Mostofit LLC. Messages may include verification codes, announcements, event reminders, donation confirmations, and urgent community updates.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. Users may reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell user personal information. We may share information only with trusted service providers who help operate the app, website, messaging services, payment processing, hosting, analytics, or support functions.

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

## 7. User Choices

Users may update their account information, change notification preferences, or opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on the official website or app.
