# AIT-Houston Child Safety Standards Policy (CSAE)

**Effective Date:** June 2026
**Last Updated:** June 2026

## 1. Our Commitment

AIT-Houston is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for all users. We maintain a **zero-tolerance policy** toward any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

We prohibit any content, behavior, communication, or activity that exploits, abuses, endangers, or sexualizes minors.

This policy applies to all users, members, administrators, businesses, organizations, and partners using the AIT-Houston mobile application, website, and related services.

---

## 2. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on AIT-Houston:

### Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

* Uploading, sharing, storing, distributing, requesting, or promoting CSAM.
* Linking to websites or services containing CSAM.
* Attempting to obtain or exchange CSAM.

### Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

* Grooming or attempting to establish inappropriate relationships with minors.
* Soliciting sexual content from minors.
* Sexual extortion ("sextortion") involving minors.
* Human trafficking or exploitation involving minors.
* Any communication intended to sexually exploit, abuse, or endanger a child.
* Any behavior that violates child protection laws.

### Misrepresentation

* Falsifying age or identity to interact with minors.
* Creating accounts on behalf of minors without proper authorization.
* Using the platform to facilitate harmful contact with minors.

---

## 3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users who encounter content or behavior that may violate this policy should report it immediately.

### In-App Reporting

Users may report:

* Inappropriate messages
* Suspicious profiles
* Illegal content
* Harassment involving minors
* Potential CSAE or CSAM violations

Reports are reviewed promptly by authorized administrators.

### Emergency Situations

If a child is in immediate danger, users should contact local law enforcement authorities immediately.

---

## 4. Enforcement Actions

AIT-Houston reserves the right to take any of the following actions when violations are detected:

* Immediate content removal
* Account suspension
* Permanent account termination
* Restriction of platform access
* Preservation of evidence
* Reporting to law enforcement agencies
* Reporting to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or other applicable authorities when required by law

AIT-Houston may cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations involving CSAE or CSAM.

---

## 5. Child Protection and Compliance

AIT-Houston complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including:

* Google Play Child Safety Standards
* Apple App Store child safety requirements
* U.S. federal and state child protection laws
* Other applicable international child protection regulations

We regularly review our policies and moderation processes to ensure compliance with evolving legal and platform requirements.

---

## 6. User Responsibilities

All users must:

* Respect the safety and dignity of minors.
* Report suspected CSAE or CSAM immediately.
* Refrain from sharing content that exploits or endangers children.
* Comply with all applicable laws and platform policies.

Failure to comply may result in account termination and referral to appropriate authorities.

---

## 7. Privacy and Confidentiality

Reports related to child safety are handled confidentially whenever possible and in accordance with applicable laws.

Information may be disclosed to law enforcement or child protection agencies when legally required or necessary to protect a child from harm.

---

## 8. Child Safety Point of Contact

For child safety concerns, CSAE reports, or compliance inquiries, please contact:

**AIT-Houston Child Safety Team**
Email: [safety@ait-houston.org](mailto:safety@ait-houston.org)

If reporting content, please include:

* Username or profile involved
* Description of the concern
* Screenshots or evidence (if available)
* Date and time of the incident

---

## 9. Policy Updates

AIT-Houston may update this policy periodically to reflect legal, regulatory, and platform requirements.

Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

---

## 10. Zero-Tolerance Statement

AIT-Houston maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any violation of this policy will result in immediate action, including removal of content, termination of accounts, and reporting to appropriate authorities.

Together, we can help create a safe and trusted environment for our community.
